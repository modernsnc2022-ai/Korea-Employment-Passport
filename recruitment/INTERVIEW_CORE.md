# Broker replacement interview

Privacy rule: record the participant only by their assigned `KEP-####` beta ID in this repository. Do not record names, contact details, passport/KTP/ARC numbers, exact addresses, or private intermediary identities here. Capture the **task/problem**, not the person's identity.

Ask every participant:
1. What stage are you in now?
2. Which official websites/accounts do you use?
3. Did you use an LPK, helper, agent, broker, friend, or paid intermediary?
4. Exactly what did that person do for you?
5. How much did you pay, item by item?
6. Which tasks could you not have completed alone?
7. Which form fields or documents were confusing?
8. Which deadlines or announcements were hard to find?
9. Were you ever told payment could improve employer selection speed?
10. Were you promised a Korean employer or SLC?
11. Which claims did you verify with KP2MI/HRD Korea?
12. What mistakes almost caused rejection or delay?
13. What do people in your community commonly misunderstand?
14. Which app feature would let you stop using the intermediary?
15. Would you trust an app more if every answer linked to an official source?
16. Would company photos/videos before accepting SLC matter to you?
17. Would verified-worker reviews matter?
18. Would you pay for a physical workplace visit? How much?
19. What should always remain free?
20. After seeing the prototype: is there any reason you still need a broker?

PASS answer:
"No private broker is needed for any remaining task."
