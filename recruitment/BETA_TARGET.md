# Indonesia beta cohort

## Public beta: initial validation target of at least 30 active applicants

Application intake may open **before** beta access is approved. The purpose is to see actual demand first, then approve eligible testers when the product is ready.

Policy:
- target at least **30** active Indonesian applicants for the validation cohort;
- 30 is a validation target, not an automatic rejection cap;
- do not reject an otherwise eligible applicant only because the count becomes 31 or slightly higher;
- KEP-0001..KEP-0030 are the base active-applicant cohort;
- KEP-0031..KEP-0050 remain reserved for the separate E-9 worker retrospective panel;
- additional approved active applicants use KEP-0051 and upward, contiguously;
- approval/activation happens only after the beta-access launch gate passes and an explicit release decision is recorded;
- applications received while access is still HOLD remain eligible for later review;
- each approved tester receives Korea Employment Passport service **free for 6 months from beta account activation**;
- the benefit is conditional on good-faith product feedback;
- no payment is required to apply or participate;
- beta participation is not a job sale, placement service, employer-selection guarantee, SLC guarantee, visa guarantee, or departure guarantee.

Expected feedback participation:
- complete first-use feedback after access is approved;
- report any step where the app is unclear, missing an official answer, rejected, or still requires a calo/private intermediary;
- provide short follow-up feedback when the real EPS stage changes, when reasonably possible.

A participant does not have to disclose passport/KTP/ARC numbers or other sensitive identity documents to apply or receive the beta benefit.

## Separate validation panel: 20 E-9 workers already in Korea

Target B remains 20 Indonesians already working in Korea on E-9.

This group is a **retrospective validation panel**, separate from the active-applicant cohort. Their role is to check whether instructions, document expectations, timing, workplace reality information, and broker-replacement claims match actual experience.

## Primary success metric

Broker Replacement Rate = broker tasks no longer needed / all broker tasks encountered.

PASS condition for supported route:
- 100% of necessary replaceable private-broker tasks replaced;
- official-authority steps clearly routed;
- licensed-professional steps, if any, completed without private broker hunting.

## Beta ID and privacy rule

Use pseudonymous KEP codes only in this repository.
- KEP-0001–KEP-0030: base active-applicant validation cohort.
- KEP-0031–KEP-0050: Indonesian E-9 workers in Korea / retrospective validation panel.
- KEP-0051 and upward: additional approved active applicants beyond the initial 30-person target.
- The app accepts the assigned code locally and includes it in sanitized beta feedback so reports can be matched to the tracker.
- Do **not** commit participant names, nicknames/handles, phone numbers, email addresses, passport/KTP/ARC numbers, exact home addresses, or private intermediary names to this public repository.
- If direct contact details are needed for application review/follow-up, keep them in a separate access-controlled communication system keyed only by tester ID after approval.
- Free-text tracker notes must remain non-identifying.
