# Indonesia beta cohort

## Public beta: first 30 active applicants

Public beta recruitment stays **closed** until `BETA_LAUNCH_GATE.md` passes.

When the gate passes:
- recruit the first **30** eligible people who are actively going through Indonesia -> Korea G-to-G / EPS E-9 process, ordered by the received timestamp of applications submitted after recruitment becomes OPEN;
- each accepted tester receives Korea Employment Passport service **free for 6 months from beta account activation**;
- the benefit is conditional on good-faith participation in product feedback;
- this is a product beta, not a job sale, placement service, visa guarantee, or departure guarantee;
- no payment is required to join the beta.

Expected feedback participation:
- complete first-use feedback after trying the service;
- report any step where the app is unclear, missing an official answer, or still makes the user seek a calo/private intermediary;
- provide short follow-up feedback when their real EPS stage changes, when reasonably possible.

A participant does not have to disclose passport/KTP/ARC numbers or other sensitive identity documents to receive the beta benefit.

## Separate validation panel: 20 E-9 workers already in Korea

Target B remains 20 Indonesians already working in Korea on E-9.

This group is a **retrospective validation panel**, separate from the 30-person public beta allocation. Their role is to check whether instructions, document expectations, timing, workplace reality information, and broker-replacement claims match actual experience.

## Primary success metric

Broker Replacement Rate = broker tasks no longer needed / all broker tasks encountered.

PASS condition for supported route:
- 100% of necessary private-broker tasks replaced;
- official-authority steps clearly routed;
- licensed-professional steps, if any, completed without private broker hunting.

## Beta ID and privacy rule

Use only the pseudonymous codes `KEP-0001` through `KEP-0050` in this repository.
- `KEP-0001`–`KEP-0030`: first-30 public beta active applicants.
- `KEP-0031`–`KEP-0050`: Indonesian E-9 workers in Korea / retrospective validation panel.
- The app accepts the code locally and includes it in sanitized beta feedback so reports can be matched to the tracker.
- Do **not** commit participant names, nicknames/handles, phone numbers, email addresses, passport/KTP/ARC numbers, exact home addresses, or private intermediary names to this public repository.
- If direct contact details are needed for recruitment/follow-up, keep them in a separate access-controlled system keyed only by `tester_id`.
- Free-text tracker notes must remain non-identifying.
