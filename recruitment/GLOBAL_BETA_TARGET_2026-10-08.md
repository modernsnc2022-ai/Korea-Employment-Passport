# Global beta interest expansion — 2026-10-08

## Purpose

Accept validator / beta-test interest from supported non-Indonesia Manufacturing Country Packs without claiming that those routes are beta-ready.

## Intake policy

- Indonesia keeps its existing dedicated beta intake.
- Non-Indonesia countries are admitted to **interest registration** when the readiness dashboard has a registered route and `packState=research_hold`.
- Interest registration does **not** activate beta access.
- Approval remains manual and country-specific.
- No applicant is asked for passport, national ID, ARC, identity number, home address, or document images.
- Myanmar is excluded until direct 2026 Manufacturing evidence is verified.
- Kazakhstan is excluded because operational EPS introduction is planned for 2028.
- Country-specific exact rules must never be inferred from another country pack.

## First outreach wave

Prioritize countries with comparatively strong 2026 Manufacturing evidence and useful later-stage validation:

1. Cambodia (KH)
2. Uzbekistan (UZ)
3. Mongolia (MN)
4. Tajikistan (TJ)

Initial target: 5 qualified active applicants per country. This is a validation target, not an automatic rejection cap.

## Second wave

Vietnam (VN), Thailand (TH), and Sri Lanka (LK) after their scheduled October official-review triggers are checked.

## Remaining registered research-hold countries

Nepal (NP), Philippines (PH), Bangladesh (BD), Laos (LA), China (CN), Timor-Leste (TL), Pakistan (PK), Kyrgyzstan (KG).

## Public intake

`docs/beta-global.html`

The page reads the current readiness dashboard and only offers countries with a registered non-Indonesia route in `research_hold`. Broad stage categories are used at intake so the form does not invent country-specific step mappings.
