### January 27 is important: “lamaran online” does not supply the final file checklist

The **January 27, 2026** official general recruitment body does mention `lamaran online` three times. Read in context, the clause concerns **MCU I**, **HIMPSI psychological assessment**, entry of results directly into SiskoP2MI by medical/HIMPSI providers, and different handling of applicants who graduated before 2026. These process facts were already represented as baseline evidence in the product.

The January notice separately gives **February 2026 EPS-TOPIK examination-registration** scan requirements (ID card, passport if held, photo) and supporting registration manuals. Those **cannot** be relabeled as the precise later **post-Skill Test, final 2026 General Manufacturing online job-application** list of mandatory uploads.

The **September 17** notice is **optional** Job Capacity Assessment / Skill Test competency supporting documents, was uploaded through SiskoP2MI **September 21 at 09:00 to September 23 at 13:00 WIB** (now closed), and is also **not** the final job-application checklist. Distinguish `Skill Test wajib` from `Job Capacity Assessment opsional`.

Other notice exclusions remain: August 2026 Fisheries online job-application, September 2026 Root Industry online job-application and December 2025 General Manufacturing online job-application are **different cohort/year/sector evidence** and cannot be copied into 2026 General Manufacturing `job_docs`.
