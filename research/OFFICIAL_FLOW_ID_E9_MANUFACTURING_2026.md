# Indonesia E-9 Manufacturing 2026 — verified flow

Status: baseline under active verification
Target: private broker required = 0

1. Recruitment announcement / eligibility
2. Online registration through Sisko P2MI
3. Biometric fingerprint + photo capture
4. Online document verification
5. HRD Korea candidate validation / exam card
6. EPS-TOPIK
7. Skill Test + Competency Test
8. Final selection
9. Online job application / job-seeker roster
10. Korean employer selection
11. Standard Labour Contract (SLC)
12. Contract review / accept or cancel
13. Psychology examination as applicable
14. MCU II / visa document preparation
15. OPP (pre-departure orientation)
16. Visa processing
17. Pre-departure confirmation / Q-Code / Arrival Card guidance
18. Departure to Korea

Important:
- EPS-TOPIK pass alone does not guarantee employment.
- Official employer selection is not a private-broker service.
- 2026 manufacturing selection uses point-system ranking.
- Mutable rules must be checked against the current KP2MI/HRD Korea notice.
