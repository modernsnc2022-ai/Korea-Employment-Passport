## Official product candidate

**BNI KUR Penempatan Pekerja Migran Indonesia (KUR PMI)**

Official BNI product information states:
- maximum credit: up to Rp100,000,000;
- interest: 6% effective per year;
- tenor: aligned to employment contract, maximum 3 years;
- collateral: not required;
- listed destination countries include **Korea Selatan**.

2026 KUR implementation rules also include Korea Selatan in the country-level KUR PMI reporting framework.

## Important qualification / product guardrail

Do **not** tell an EPS/G-to-G Korea applicant that financing is guaranteed or immediately available.

BNI's published KUR PMI documentation requires placement/employment-related documents and credit review. Some published BNI materials are written around P3MI placement documents, while Korea G-to-G placement is government-to-government. Therefore KEP must present this as:

> "Official financing option to check with BNI; eligibility depends on the applicant's current EPS/G-to-G stage and BNI's document/credit review."

Do not infer eligibility from exam-passed, roster, or pre-contract status alone.
