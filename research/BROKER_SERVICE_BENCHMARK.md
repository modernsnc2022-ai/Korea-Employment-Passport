# Broker / helper service benchmark — Indonesia E-9

Purpose: identify every practical task that currently causes applicants to depend on helpers, LPKs or informal intermediaries.

## Publicly observed helper topics

### INFO KERJA KOREA / Korean First
- EPS-TOPIK preparation
- process guidance from early preparation toward Korea employment
- sending-data document checklist
- Sisko P2MI account/process reminders
- passport / MCU related guidance
- consultation around official process

Observed public sending checklist includes:
KTP, KK, diploma, color-vision document, passport, MCU, family/spouse permission, birth certificate, AK1/yellow card.

## Product implication
Every helper task above must map to one of:
- App replaces it completely
- App guides user through official authority
- App verifies current official requirements and warns when a public helper list is stale

Important: public helper lists are discovery evidence, not authoritative requirements.
Official current-cycle KP2MI/HRD Korea notices always override them.
