# Source policy

Priority order:
1. HRD Korea / EPS / EPS-TOPIK
2. KP2MI official G-to-G announcements
3. Korean immigration / labor authorities
4. Official partner documents
5. Verified worker evidence
6. Community reports (never treated as official fact)

Every mutable rule stores:
- source URL
- publication date
- effective date if known
- country
- visa route
- sector
- recruitment cycle
- verification status
- reviewer
- last checked date

AI explanations must never overwrite official facts.
